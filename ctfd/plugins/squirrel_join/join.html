How the game works

The Chippewa squirrels are locked in Acorn Hall. Your team races the other side to let them out.

  1. 1 Open the door

    Read Squirrel Door Note. Type the PIN on the building. The door opens and you get a flag.

  2. 2 Free the squirrels

    Read Snack Closet Log before you walk in. Type that code. Three wrong tries, or the timer, and the squirrels scatter.

  3. 3 Win the race

    Turn both flags in on the scoreboard. The first team through both flags wins.
